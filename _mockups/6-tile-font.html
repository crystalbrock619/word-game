Press Start 2PMOST ARCADE

Actual 8-bit arcade lettering. Pixel grids cannot make two glyphs similar — D is a hard-edged block, O is open. The most on-theme option by a distance. Costs the most room per glyph, so tiles run smaller, and it is the least readable of the five in long words.
