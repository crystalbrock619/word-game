tier wordmarks — played when a word is served, then repeated at random intervals

tier 1 · letters trade places a few times, then settle off-baseline

tier 2 · the twist travels down the word, letters turning edge-on at the turn

tier 3 · letters fly in from both sides, collide, and squash on impact

tier 4 · the word holds intact for a beat, then blows apart — fireball flares on the blast and burns out with it, smoke lingers

Pure CSS keyframes on live text. Run time 1000ms; repeat at a 9s base randomised ±40%, so the rhythm never becomes a metronome. Every animation ends on the static wordmark, so play is never gated on it finishing.

Under prefers-reduced-motion the animations are dropped entirely and the static wordmarks show instead.
